Build programs, agents, bots, and tools on the BetDEX API. Get paid when you ship. Live through Dec 31. Up to \$50,000 in bounties. Ongoing revenue share for anything that trades.

BetDEX is the exchange built for programmatic order flow in sports. We’re running a rolling builder program through the end of the year to reward developers who ship real, useful integrations, agents, and tools on our API.

Pick a bounty from the open board, propose a build, ship it, get paid. If you keep it live and it drives trading volume, we keep paying you.

​

### What you’ll build

The board lists specific bounties we want built, with clear scope and payout. You can also submit an open proposal (RFP-style) for something we haven’t listed. Strong submissions typically involve:

- Real trading, market-making, or agent workflows on the BetDEX API
- Tool use, MCP integration, or API orchestration
- A working demo or live deployment we can inspect

### Example bounties on the board

- **Price Comparison tool** - how does BetDEX odds stack up against others?
- **BetDEX MCP server** — lets AI agents (Claude, GPT, etc.) trade on BetDEX through a standard tool interface
- ​**Flumine adapter** — open-source integration so every existing flumine user can point their Betfair bot at BetDEX
- **Natural-language trading agent** — "lay Liverpool at any odds above 2.5" → executed automatically
- **News-to-trade agent** — monitors injury reports, lineups, weather; opens positions before the market reacts
- **In-play market-making bot** — two-sided quoting with inventory and risk management
- **Cross-venue arbitrage agent** — monitors BetDEX vs Betfair, Polymarket, Kalshi and executes on price gaps
- **"My personal bookie" agent** — an agent that takes betting instructions and recycles winnings per your rules
- **Public dashboards / analytics tools** — volume, liquidity, market discovery for other traders
- **LangChain / CrewAI / Vercel AI SDK integrations** — make BetDEX a first-class tool in the popular agent frameworks

​Have an idea that’s not listed? Submit an open proposal (see below) and we’ll green-light and price it.

### How it works

1. **<u>Browse the bounty board</u>** <br />Live at developers.betdex.com/bounties. Each bounty lists scope, acceptance criteria, payout, and any existing builders already claimed on it.<br />
2. **<u>Claim a bounty or submit an open proposal</u>** <br />Claim — short form: who you are, GitHub, rough approach, expected timeline. We approve or discuss within 3 business days. Multiple builders can work on the same bounty in parallel unless it’s marked exclusive. <br /><br />Open proposal — pitch something not on the board. Include the problem, the build, why it matters, and a suggested payout. We respond within 5 business days: green-light with a payout, negotiate scope, or pass.<br />
3. **<u>Build in the sandbox</u>** <br />You get sandbox API keys, pre-funded test accounts, and access to our engineering team in Discord for questions.<br />
4. **<u>Submit for review</u>** <br />Push code \+ demo video or live link to your bounty page. Our team reviews against the acceptance criteria and either accepts, requests changes, or (rarely) declines with reasoning.<br />
5. **<u>Get paid</u>** <br />Bounty payouts in USDC within 10 days of acceptance. If your build produces ongoing trading volume, you’re also enrolled in the Builder Revenue Share (below).

### Ongoing revenue share

​Anything that keeps trading on BetDEX after acceptance also earns you a cut of the fees, for 12 months from launch, tiered by volume:

- \$0–\$500k volume: 10% of fees back
- \$500k–\$5m volume: 15% of fees back
- \$5m\+ volume: 20% of fees back

​Public tools that bring in new traders (SDKs, integrations, tutorials) also qualify for a 25% affiliate share on fees from any trader they source, ongoing as long as the tool is maintained.
